# Code of Conduct

NestWall is a family project, and we want contributing to it to be a welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- Keep discussions on the work, not on the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub (the repository's **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**, or contact the maintainer's GitHub profile). The maintainer will review the report and may remove comments, commits or contributors who break these rules.
